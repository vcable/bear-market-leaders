Daily closes and volume, split-adjusted, from Massive. SPY and QQQ are rebased to the stock's close on its low, so the gap between the lines is performance since that day. Volume bars are shares traded each day, the line through them is the average of the prior 50 sessions, and the callouts give volume on the low and first-new-high days as a multiple of that average. The RS line is the stock divided by SPY, set to 100 on the low. A new 52-week high is a close above every close of the prior 251 trading days; the RS line's new high uses the same rule. Shaded bands are SPY's two pullbacks inside the uptrend.
